Who We Are

More Than a Venue — An Experience

Isolomzi Place is a one-of-a-kind event and hospitality destination located at 59 Hawthorn Street, Palm Ridge, Katlehong. With thatched-roof structures, lush trees, a sparkling pool, and multiple event spaces, we bring people together for life's biggest moments.

Whether you're planning a corporate conference, a birthday celebration, a wedding, or just a relaxed pool day — Isolomzi Place has the space, the atmosphere, and the heart to make it memorable.
